# 2. Executive Summary

UrbanMart is a fictional U.S.-based retail organization operating multiple stores and digital sales channels.

The business identified challenges across sales transactions, payments, inventory, returns, customer information, loyalty activity, and financial reconciliation.

The objective of this project was to analyze the existing business processes, identify business requirements, design improved future-state processes, create a structured data model, perform data analysis, validate the proposed solution through UAT, and establish complete requirement traceability.

The project was developed from a **Business Analyst perspective**, with supporting SQL, data modeling, reporting, and testing activities.

---

# 3. Business Problem

UrbanMart needed better visibility and control across its retail operations.

Key business challenges included:

- Inconsistent customer information.
- Incomplete sales transactions.
- Payment exceptions.
- Product and inventory visibility issues.
- Return-processing inconsistencies.
- Loyalty transaction tracking challenges.
- Promotion management issues.
- Store-level performance visibility.
- POS and payment reconciliation differences.
- Data-quality and referential-integrity concerns.

These challenges made it difficult for business stakeholders to consistently monitor operational and financial performance.

---

# 4. Project Objective

The primary objective was to design a structured business-analysis solution that would help UrbanMart:

- Improve transaction visibility.
- Improve payment monitoring.
- Improve inventory visibility.
- Improve return tracking.
- Improve customer data quality.
- Support loyalty analysis.
- Improve reconciliation.
- Establish consistent business rules.
- Support data-driven decision making.
- Validate requirements through UAT.
- Maintain end-to-end requirement traceability.

---

# 5. Business Questions

The project was designed around practical business questions.

### Sales

- How much did each store sell?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which categories perform best?
- What is the transaction volume?
- Are there incomplete transactions?

### Customers

- How many active and inactive customers exist?
- What customer segments exist?
- Is customer data complete?

### Inventory

- Which products are low in stock?
- Which products are out of stock?
- Which stores have inventory concerns?

### Returns

- What is the return rate?
- Which products are frequently returned?
- How many returns are rejected?
- Are returned quantities valid?

### Payments

- Which payment methods are used?
- Are there incomplete payments?
- Are payment amounts consistent with transactions?

### Reconciliation

- Does POS total match payment total?
- Which stores have reconciliation variances?
- What exceptions require investigation?
