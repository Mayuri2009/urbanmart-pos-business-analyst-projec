# UrbanMart POS — UAT Results and Sign-off

[05_UAT_Results_and_Signoff.docx](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/31518107/05_UAT_Results_and_Signoff.docx)

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to summarize the UrbanMart POS User Acceptance Testing results and provide a structured approach for business acceptance and sign-off.

The document brings together:

- UAT execution results
- Test case status
- Defect status
- Business rule validation
- Exception validation
- Requirement coverage
- Acceptance criteria
- Final business decision
- UAT sign-off

---

# 2. UAT Completion Objective

The objective of the final UAT assessment is to determine whether the UrbanMart POS solution is acceptable from a business perspective.

The final assessment follows:

```text
UAT Test Execution
        ↓
Test Results
        ↓
Defect Review
        ↓
Retesting
        ↓
Requirement Validation
        ↓
Acceptance Criteria Review
        ↓
Business Decision
        ↓
Sign-off
