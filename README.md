# UrbanMart POS — Business Analyst Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

UrbanMart is a fictional U.S.-based retail company operating multiple stores and sales channels.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Analyst workflow for analyzing and improving a Point-of-Sale (POS) environment.

The project covers business requirements analysis, stakeholder identification, process analysis, data modeling, PostgreSQL SQL analysis, Power BI reporting, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), Requirement Traceability Matrix (RTM), and business recommendations.

The project uses a synthetic retail dataset covering January 2025 through December 2025.

---

## Business Problem

UrbanMart's management needs better visibility into sales performance, product performance, inventory availability, customer activity, returns, payment exceptions, loyalty activity, and store-level reconciliation.

The existing operational data contains information across multiple business processes, making it difficult for management to quickly identify:

- High- and low-performing stores
- Top-performing products and categories
- Inventory shortages
- Customer and loyalty patterns
- Return trends and rejected returns
- Incomplete payment transactions
- Reconciliation exceptions
- Sales performance across channels

The objective of this project is to design a structured analytical solution that converts operational POS data into actionable business insights.

---

## Business Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Analyze sales performance across UrbanMart stores.
2. Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
3. Monitor inventory availability and stock risks.
4. Analyze customer and loyalty activity.
5. Understand return trends and rejected returns.
6. Identify incomplete payment transactions.
7. Analyze payment reconciliation exceptions.
8. Compare Store, Web, and Mobile sales channels.
9. Provide management with actionable business insights.
10. Demonstrate an end-to-end Business Analyst project lifecycle.

| Incomplete Transactions | 2.51% |
