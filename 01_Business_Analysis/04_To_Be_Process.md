# UrbanMart POS — To-Be Process

## 1. Future-State Objective

The To-Be process is designed to create a structured flow from operational POS data to business insights.

The future-state solution connects business requirements, data modeling, PostgreSQL, SQL analysis, Power BI, and UAT.
