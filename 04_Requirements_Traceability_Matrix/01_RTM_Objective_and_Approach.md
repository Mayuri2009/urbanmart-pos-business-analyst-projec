## 1. Purpose

The purpose of the Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) is to provide end-to-end traceability between UrbanMart business requirements and the supporting analysis, design, data, SQL, UAT, defects, and final business acceptance.

The RTM ensures that important business requirements are not lost during the project lifecycle.

---

# 2. RTM Objective

The primary objective of the RTM is to ensure that every important UrbanMart business requirement can be traced from its origin through validation and final acceptance.

The traceability flow is:

```text
Business Requirement
        ↓
BRD Requirement
        ↓
User Story
        ↓
Use Case
        ↓
Data Model / Business Rule
        ↓
SQL Validation
        ↓
Power BI / Business Analysis
        ↓
UAT Scenario
        ↓
UAT Test Case
        ↓
UAT Result
        ↓
Defect
        ↓
Retest
        ↓
Final Status
