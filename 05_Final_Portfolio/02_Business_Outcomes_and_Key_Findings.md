## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to summarize the key business outcomes and findings identified through the UrbanMart POS Business Analyst project.

The analysis connects business requirements, data analysis, SQL validation, reporting requirements, UAT findings, and operational observations to demonstrate the business value of the proposed solution.

---

# 2. Business Outcome Objective

The project aims to provide UrbanMart stakeholders with better visibility into:

- Sales performance
- Store performance
- Product performance
- Customer activity
- Inventory availability
- Returns
- Payments
- Loyalty
- Promotions
- Reconciliation
- Data quality
- Operational exceptions
