## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to clearly define the Business Analyst role, responsibilities, activities, and contributions performed throughout the UrbanMart POS project.

The objective is to demonstrate how the Business Analyst acted as the bridge between business stakeholders, requirements, data, technical analysis, testing, and business outcomes.
