# UrbanMart POS — RTM Final Validation

[04_RTM_Final_Validation.docx](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/31529537/04_RTM_Final_Validation.docx)

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to perform the final validation of the UrbanMart POS Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM).

The final validation confirms that business requirements are properly connected to the relevant project artifacts and that sufficient evidence exists to support business acceptance.

---

# 2. Final RTM Validation Objective

The objective is to confirm that:

- Requirements are complete.
- Requirements are uniquely identified.
- Requirements are traceable.
- Business rules are covered.
- Data requirements are covered.
- SQL analysis is linked where applicable.
- Power BI analysis is linked where applicable.
- UAT coverage exists.
- Defects are traceable.
- Retesting is completed where required.
- Final requirement statuses are accurate.
