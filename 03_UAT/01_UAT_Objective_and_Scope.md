## 1. Purpose

The purpose of User Acceptance Testing (UAT) is to validate whether the UrbanMart POS solution meets the agreed business requirements and supports the expected retail business processes.

UAT focuses on **business acceptance**, ensuring that the solution produces the expected business outcomes rather than testing only technical functionality.

---

# 2. UAT Objective

The primary objective of UAT is to confirm that the UrbanMart POS solution:

- Meets approved business requirements.
- Supports defined business processes.
- Applies agreed business rules correctly.
- Produces accurate business results.
- Handles expected business exceptions.
- Provides reliable data for business analysis.
- Is acceptable to business stakeholders.

The UAT flow is:

```text
Business Requirement
        ↓
Business Scenario
        ↓
UAT Test Case
        ↓
Test Execution
        ↓
Expected vs Actual Result
        ↓
Pass / Fail
        ↓
Defect / Retest
        ↓
Business Acceptance
