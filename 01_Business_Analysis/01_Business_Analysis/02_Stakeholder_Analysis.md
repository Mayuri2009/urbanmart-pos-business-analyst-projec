# UrbanMart POS — Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Stakeholder Identification

The UrbanMart POS analytical solution involves stakeholders from business operations, finance, inventory, marketing, technology, and management.

Understanding stakeholder needs is important because each stakeholder group uses the information differently.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Role | Key Interest | Influence | Information Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Senior Management | Strategic decision-making | Overall business performance | High | Revenue, profitability indicators, store performance, trends |
| Store Manager | Store operations | Store-level performance | High | Sales, transactions, inventory, returns |
| Regional Manager | Regional oversight | Store comparison | High | Regional sales, store ranking, operational exceptions |
| Finance Team | Financial control | Payments and reconciliation | High | Payment status, payment totals, reconciliation variance |
| Inventory Team | Inventory management | Product availability | High | Stock levels, low stock, out-of-stock products |
| Marketing / CRM Team | Customer engagement | Loyalty and customer behavior | Medium | Customer segments, loyalty activity, promotions |
| Operations Team | POS operations | Transaction processing | High | Incomplete transactions, transaction volume, exceptions |
| IT / Data Team | Data infrastructure | Data quality and reporting | Medium | Data model, database, SQL, data validation |

---

## 3. Stakeholder Needs

### Senior Management

Senior management needs a high-level view of business performance.

Key questions:

- How much did UrbanMart sell?
- Which stores performed best?
- Which channels performed best?
- What operational risks require attention?

---

### Store Managers

Store managers need detailed visibility into individual store performance.

Key questions:

- How much did my store sell?
- Which products sell the most?
- Are important products out of stock?
- How many returns occurred?
- Are there transaction exceptions?

---

### Regional Managers

Regional managers need to compare stores and identify regional patterns.

Key questions:

- Which stores outperform their region?
- Which stores require attention?
- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Are inventory issues concentrated in specific locations?

---

### Finance Team

Finance requires accurate payment and reconciliation information.

Key questions:

- How many payments are incomplete?
- What is the payment variance?
- Which stores have reconciliation exceptions?
- Are POS totals aligned with payment totals?

---

### Inventory Team

The inventory team needs visibility into stock availability.

Key questions:

- Which products are low in stock?
- Which products are out of stock?
- Which stores have the highest inventory risk?

---

### Marketing / CRM Team

Marketing and CRM teams need customer and loyalty information.

Key questions:

- Which customer segments are most active?
- How is the loyalty program performing?
- Which customers are inactive?
- How can promotions be better targeted?

---

## 4. Stakeholder Priority

Stakeholders are prioritized based on business impact and influence.

### High Priority

- Senior Management
- Store Managers
- Regional Managers
- Finance Team
- Inventory Team
- Operations Team

### Medium Priority

- Marketing / CRM Team
- IT / Data Team

| Stakeholder | Communication |
|---|---|
| Senior Management | Executive dashboard and summary |
| Store Manager | Store-level dashboard |
| Regional Manager | Regional performance dashboard |
| Finance | Payment and reconciliation reports |
| Inventory Team | Inventory exception reports |
| Marketing / CRM | Customer and loyalty analysis |
| Operations | Transaction exception reporting |
| IT / Data | Data model, SQL and validation documentation |

---

## 6. Business Analyst Responsibility

The Business Analyst is responsible for:

- Identifying stakeholders
- Understanding stakeholder objectives
- Eliciting business requirements
- Resolving conflicting requirements
- Translating business needs into functional requirements
- Maintaining requirement traceability
- Supporting UAT
- Ensuring the final analytical solution addresses business needs
