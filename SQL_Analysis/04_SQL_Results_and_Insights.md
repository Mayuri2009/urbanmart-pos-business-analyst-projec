# UrbanMart POS — SQL Results & Business Insights

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to convert SQL query results into meaningful business insights.

SQL provides the numerical result.

Business analysis explains:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What business risk or opportunity exists?
- What action should management consider?

The analysis in this document is based on the 15 priority business questions defined in `02_Business_Questions.sql` and implemented in `03_SQL_Queries.sql`.

---

The SQL queries must be executed against the UrbanMart PostgreSQL database before recording actual numerical results.
